Overlays, textures and lighting effects that give a flat screen depth and material: grain, color gradients, print dots, hand-drawn strokes, device frames and spotlights.

Grain / Noise texture

Grain: a fine noise layer over a solid color or gradient that reduces the flatness of digital graphics and makes them feel like printed paper or film, warmer and more tactile.

Gradient mesh / Aurora

Gradient mesh, or aurora effect: several color shapes are blurred, blend into each other and drift very slowly. Tap the button in the frame to see the original shapes before the blur.

Halftone
